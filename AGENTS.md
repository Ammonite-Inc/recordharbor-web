# Record Harbor

Record Harbor is real estate transaction middleware, powered by Ammonite Intelligence.

The legal name of the company is Ammonite Intelligence, Inc.

## What it is

From contract to close, Record Harbor is one secure file for everyone at the table, with the public record already in it. The aim is fewer surprises, fewer status calls, and a smoother closing day.

Deeds, mortgages, liens, plats, permits and tax rolls sit in county systems that do not talk to each other. Record Harbor translates them into one clean record of every parcel and puts it in the deal file.

The product is organized around four pillars, all in one universal data schema:

- Secure Document Intelligence
- Public Records Access
- Private Order Repositories
- Folder Structures

The public homepage shows product previews drawn from sample data. Names, places and numbers are made up. The previews cover parcel lineage (a parcel’s family tree and chain of title), document intelligence (deeds and mortgages read for parties, legal descriptions and amounts), folders and access by role, and the Transaction Auditor (one event log across the deal, the closing and the public record, in the exact order things happened).

It is meant to serve buyers and sellers, agents and brokers, title, escrow and attorneys, lenders, and developers, builders and surveyors.

Footer line used on the site: Real estate, simplified from contract to close. Public records remain their publishers’.
