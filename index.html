Why it changes the game

The public record, translated.

Deeds, mortgages, liens, plats, permits and tax rolls sit in county systems that don’t talk to each other. Record Harbor translates them into one clean record of every parcel and puts it in the deal file. The unreleased mortgage, the old lien, the line that moved: you see it at contract, not at the closing table.

One parcel. Every record. Every party.

Parcel lineage

Every parcel’s family tree.

Follow a parcel from its first patent through every split, merge and plat to the unit under contract today. Each link names the recorded document behind it.

  • Splits, merges and renumbering, dated
  • Chain of title, deed by deed
  • Open mortgages flagged before closing

Document intelligence

Deeds and mortgages, read for you.

Drop in a recorded document, or let Record Harbor pull it from the county. The parties, legal description and amounts come out ready to check against the commitment.

  • Grantor, grantee, lender and amount pulled out
  • Legal description matched to the parcel
  • Each document tied to its Schedule B item

Folders and access

Folders your team already knows.

Portfolio, project, unit, file. Every party gets their own folder view and sees only the documents meant for them, and every file says who has access.

  • Shared by role, never by email attachment
  • Buyers and sellers get a simple “My closing” folder
  • See the file exactly as each party sees it
